# Kadar

An Android film camera with six looks: CineStill 800T, Portra 800, Portra 400, Ektar 100, HP5 Plus and Gold 200.

- Photos develop on the phone. The app has no server and sends nothing to the internet.
- **Save to gallery** puts photos in **Pictures/Kadar**.
- **Google Drive** opens Drive with the photo, so it goes straight to your own account.
- Unlimited shots, smooth 1×/2× zoom, tap to focus, flash, date stamp, film border and composition grid (rule of thirds / golden ratio).
- The volume buttons take a photo, like a real camera.
- Album grouped by day: Upload photos from the gallery, Send to Google Photos / Drive / other apps, Save to gallery.
- Select several photos to send, save or delete them (tap Select, or long-press a photo).
- Preview and developing run on the GPU (WebGL): per-hue color (like a 3D LUT), organic color grain and halation around lights.
- Change the film after shooting: the original is kept, so any photo can be re-developed with another film.
- Swipe up/down on the viewfinder for exposure, 3 s / 10 s timer, screen flash for selfies, shutter and film-advance sound.

## Download

On your phone open **Releases → latest** and download `Kadar.apk`.

Every push to `main` builds a new APK (GitHub Actions).
